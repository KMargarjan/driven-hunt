THE LEAF IS MOST OF "it's broken there", AND IT WAS MEASURED BEFORE IT WAS TOUCHED. In the Forest
Test place on 2026-10-16, with the lobby built by `ForestTestBoot`:

    DoorLeaf  centre (2853.426, 156.548, -2913.856)   size 7.000 x 8.600 x 0.450
    the inner face of the south wall:  z = -2915.2
    the floor's top:                   y =  152.048   (the leaf's bottom: 152.248)

So the "open" door stood 1.34 studs INSIDE the room and 0.2 studs OFF the floorboards -- a door
hanging in mid-air in the middle of the floor -- and it is `CanCollide = false`, so a player walks
through it. `DoorBand1` and `DoorBand2` hung with it. It is also the second half of "all things not
hanging".

THE CAUSE, so it is not re-introduced
-------------------------------------
One sign. `R_y(t) * (a, 0, 0) = (a cos t, 0, -a sin t)`, so about a hinge on the EAST jamb at
`t = -LEAF_OPEN_RADIANS` a POSITIVE half-width puts the free edge at `+3.444` in world z -- north,
into the house. The leaf needed `CFrame.new(-spec.WIDTH / 2, 0, 0)`.

WHY IT IS DELETED RATHER THAN CORRECTED
---------------------------------------
Because correcting it does not produce anything worth having, and Karen's own sentence offers the
alternative ("doors can be open no doors"):

* With the sign fixed, the leaf stands at 100 degrees in the MIDDLE OF THE PORCH (centre z about
  -2920.7 against a porch deck that reaches -2925), still `CanCollide = false`, so it is a slab
  players walk through -- the same complaint moved outdoors.
* Laid FLAT against the outside wall it covers a front window at either jamb: the windows are at
  `along` +-11 and are 5 wide (x 2858.5..2863.5 and 2836.5..2841.5), while a 7-wide leaf hinged
  4.05 from the centre spans 2854.05..2861.05 to the east or 2838.95..2845.95 to the west.
* The doorway itself is unchanged and is what Karen asked for in task 177 -- "a door that you walk
  through". `DoorPostW`, `DoorPostE`, `DoorLintel` and `DoorThreshold` stay and frame it: a 7-stud
  opening, 9 studs high, with nothing in it.

THE ROLLBACK
------------
Put the two blocks above back as they are written here, with `CFrame.new(-spec.WIDTH / 2, 0, 0)`
instead of the positive half-width, and move the two south windows off `along` +-11 so the leaf has

(`lobby.spec`'s doorway case asserts the opening is EMPTY, which the leaf never satisfied anyway
once it drifted inside), and `Lobby.counterPart`, `Lobby.promptPart` and the pads read other names.
