ARCHIVED 2026-10-19, task 201 part 4 (design system E). Rule 7: never delete.

WHAT: the two colour fields of `Rifle.CONFIG.scope.eyepiece` in `src/shared/Rifle/init.luau`,
and the two matching fields of the exported `Eyepiece` type.

    rimColor = Color3.fromRGB(22, 22, 24),
    maskColor = Color3.fromRGB(0, 0, 0),

    export type Eyepiece = { ... rimColor: Color3, maskColor: Color3, ... }

WHY THEY WENT: the Designer's design system, 2026-10-17, §5 surface 20 -- "Mask stays `#000000`;
the rim goes from the flat grey of `t146-scope-aim.png` to `#0A0907` with a 1 px inner `BRASS` @
0.70 ring". One palette, one owner: the weapon keeps the tube's GEOMETRY (`diameterDeg`,
`rimThickDeg`, `maskTransparency`, `atBlend`) and `src/client/Hud/Style.luau` owns its COLOUR.
`Hud.layoutScope` now reads `Style.LOOK.SCRIM` and `Style.SCOPE_MASK`.

WHAT THE OLD VALUES WERE FOR, in the words of the comment that chose them (task 147): "near-black
like Karen's reference, not light grey ... sampled off that reference in the SHADOWED part of the
body rather than the lit top edge". Its own closing line is why this change was wanted: "IT MAY NOW
BE TOO DARK -- `t147-ship-scope.png` has the tube nearly merging with the darkened surround, which
is the cost of having no highlight."

TO PUT THEM BACK: restore the two fields and the two type lines, and in
`src/client/Hud/init.luau`'s `Hud.layoutScope` read `spec.maskColor` / `spec.rimColor` again
instead of the `Style` tokens. The inner brass ring ("Bezel") is a separate frame and can stay or
go on its own.
