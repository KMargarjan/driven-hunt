----------------------------------------------------------------- src/server/Match/Score.luau

local function pointsForZone(zone: string?, config): (number, boolean)
	local table_ = config.POINTS.KILL
	local known = zone ~= nil and table_[zone] ~= nil
	if known then
		return table_[zone :: string], true
	end
	-- Never free, and counted by the owner as unknownZoneKills: an attribute typo must show up as a
	-- number rather than as a zone that quietly scores nothing.
	return table_.unknown, false
end

	-- ...and its one caller, inside Score.kill:
	local killPoints, knownZone = pointsForZone(zone, config)
	if killer then
		credit(killer, killPoints, if knownZone then "kill-" .. tostring(zone) else "kill-unknown", function(row)
			...
		end)
	end

	-- ...and the push award, which paid EVERY credited driver:
	for userId in pairs(pushCredit or {}) do
		if userId ~= killer then
			credit(userId, config.POINTS.PUSH, "push", function(row)
				row.pushes += 1
			end)
		end
	end

----------------------------------------------------------------- WHY IT WAS RETIRED

Karen, 2026-10-10, answering her own design doc's question about how a kill is scored:
*"How many shoot and male is more worth then famale"*. And 2026-10-11: *"first place ofc more
legendary more ect"*.

A zone table cannot express that rule, because it does not know what it shot. Task 158 had already
found and written this down in `Match.CONFIG` -- *"THE CODE DOES NOT HAVE THAT RULE AND THIS CARD
WILL NOT PRETEND IT DOES ... `POINTS.KILL` scores a kill by HIT ZONE alone"* -- and left it as a
decision waiting for her. Task 160 is that decision arriving: a kill is scored by `POINTS.KIND`
(male 10, female 6, cub 2, legendary 25), off `record.kind`, and by nothing else.

THE HIT ZONE IS NOT GONE FROM THE GAME, only from the money. It still decides whether the animal
dies and how far it runs (`Boar.Wound`, `Boar.CONFIG.ZONES`), it is still on the kill record, still
drawn on the drive report's silhouette with its own ring colour, and still printed in the report's
ZONE column. What it no longer does is set a price.

THE REST OF THE TABLE MOVED WITH IT, and the reason is scale, not taste: against a 100-point head
shot, `ASSIST = 10` was a tenth and `SAFETY_PENALTY = -50` was half. Against a 10-point male they
would be a whole animal and five animals -- so one safety violation would cost more than a good
drive earns and end a player's board irrecoverably. The ratios Karen approved are what was kept:
ASSIST 3 (30% of a kill, the same as a push, which is what an assist is) and SAFETY_PENALTY -10
(one whole animal, on top of being tied to a tree). `PUSH = 25` became `BEATER_PUSH = 3` and changed
rule as well as number: it now pays the animal's LAST credit before it died, and the horn is a
second door into that credit.

HOW TO ROLL IT BACK: put the block above back in `Match.CONFIG.POINTS`, restore `pointsForZone` and
its two call sites in `Match.Score`, and point the owner's diagnostic at `POINTS.KILL` again. The
kind table and the beater-credit changes are independent of each other and of the rescale, so any
one of the three can go back alone.
