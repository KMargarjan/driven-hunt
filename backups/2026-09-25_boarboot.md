# Archived: `src/server/BoarBoot.server.luau` → `backups/2026-09-25_boarboot.server.luau.txt`

Date: 2026-09-25. Task 32 (ROADMAP 1.7a, the drive). Rule 7: archived with a note, never deleted.

## What it was

The boot line for the production boar: it asserted that `Workspace.TestArena` still matched
`Boar.CONFIG.field`, built and ran the one production `Boar.newRuntime`, spawned a boar immediately,
wired `Weapon.HitReported` into `runtime:takeHit`, and (since Task 28) turned `runtime.Downed` into
the shooter's kill marker.

## Why it moved

From Milestone 1.7a, **when a boar exists is a drive decision** (`docs/design/drive.md` §3.5 and
§6.3): the Match releases six boars over a ten-minute drive and clears them when it ends. Two
`Script`s in `ServerScriptService` have no defined order and cannot share a Luau value, so leaving
the runtime inside `BoarBoot` would have forced a singleton accessor on `Boar` — a global by another
name.

Every one of its jobs is now in `src/server/MatchBoot.server.luau`, verbatim except for the two
lines that hand the drive's answers to the weapon (`setArmingPolicy`, `setDriveLineProvider`) and
the removal of the immediate `runtime:spawn()`.

## Restoring it

Only if the drive is abandoned. Copy the `.txt` back to `src/server/BoarBoot.server.luau`, delete
`MatchBoot.server.luau`, and remember that `Boar.CONFIG.maxBoars` was raised from 4 to 8 in the same
task (carcasses occupy slots for `CARCASS_SECONDS`).
