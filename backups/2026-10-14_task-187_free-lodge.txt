WHAT CAME OUT OF THE CODE
-------------------------
1. `Lobby.TEMPLATE.LODGE = "Lodge"`, `TEMPLATE.DEER = "DeerHead"`, `TEMPLATE.BOAR = "BoarHead"`
   (src/shared/Lobby/init.luau). `TRUCK` and `TREES` stay.

2. `Lobby.CONFIG.LODGE.MODEL`, the free cabin's measured room, raycast in task 180 and quoted here
   so the measurement is not lost:

       MODEL = {
           SCALE = 1,
           YAW_RADIANS = 0,          -- delivered with its porch facing -Z
           ROOM = {                  -- relative to the model's bounding-box centre in x/z,
               CENTRE = Vector3.new(-0.1, 3.3, 5.0),   -- and to its BASE in y, before SCALE
               HALF_X = 9.8,
               HALF_Z = 9.8,
               CEILING_Y = 10.7,
           },
       }
       -- east wall x = +9.76, west x = -10.01, back z = +14.85, front z = -4.90,
       -- floor y = 3.31, ceiling y = 10.72.  10.6 studs of the model's 30.9-stud depth is porch,
       -- which is why the room's centre sits 5 studs north of the box's.

3. The `lodge()` branch that cloned it, and the `Build.roomFor(anchor, cfg, fromModel)` third
   argument that chose between the two rooms. `roomFor` now takes two arguments and has one branch.

4. Task 177's PARTS lodge, which was the fallback: a 40 x 28 x 10 footprint, 5 courses of 2-stud
   logs, a ridge at 18, 4 flat glass panes stuck on the side walls, a 4-post porch with a flat roof,
   and a skull plate with two beams over the door. Everything it did, the new lodge does with
   measurements instead of constants: the windows are cut OUT of the wall, the corners interlock,
   the plinth is stone, the roof has a real overhang and the antlers over the door are the same
   mount the trophy wall uses.

5. `Build.post(name, diameter, height, centre, color, parent)` -- a standing cylinder helper. Its
   last caller was 177's porch; the new porch uses squared timber. Kept here because the quarter-turn
   it documents is a correction this repository makes in four places:

       -- A cylinder's own length is its X, so a standing post is the lying one turned a quarter
       -- turn -- the same correction `Horn.pieces` and `Markers.ring` make.
       made.CFrame = CFrame.new(centre) * CFrame.Angles(0, 0, math.pi / 2)

6. `CONFIG.TROPHIES.MOUNTS` of task 180: three rows (deer west -3, boar east -3, deer east +3)
   carrying the two free head models with a plank behind each. Twelve rows of our own replace them.
