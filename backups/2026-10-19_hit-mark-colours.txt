WHY THEY WENT: the Designer's design system, 2026-10-17.
  §2, the one exception: "The crosshair, the hit mark and the scope reticle are not text and carry
  no slate. They are `PAPER` strokes with a 1 px `#0A0907` companion line offset 1 px down and
  right -- a drop line, not an outline."
  §5 surface 19: "coloured by kind: a hit `PAPER`, a kill `PINE_TEXT`, a wound `CLAY`. One colour
  serves all three today."
An outline is drawn on every side, so a 2 px arm wearing one is a 4 px arm that is half black.
`Style.hitMarkColor(kind)` is the one mapping now, and `Style.dropLine(mark)` makes the companion.
