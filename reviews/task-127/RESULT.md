1. `src/server/ForestTest/init.luau` / `ForestTest.releaseLine` — **the file is spawned back to front: the leader is put at the TAIL of it, so at release every follower is ahead of the leader and ahead of its own station, and the line does not form by walking.** Evidence, three facts that only work together: (a) the spawn loop lays the animals out in the direction of TRAVEL — `local toward = Vector3.new(0, 0, -1) -- the road is south of the spawn` and `local at = start + toward * (along * lineData.spacingStuds) + side * (...)`, so spawn index 1 sits at `start` and later indices sit progressively further SOUTH, i.e. further along the route; (b) the leader is spawn index 1 — `kinds = Boar.sounderKinds(count, rolls, boar, nil)` with `composition = nil` returns `makeup.SOW` at index 1 (`Boar.sounderKinds`: `elseif index == 1 then table.insert(kinds, makeup.SOW)`, `SOW = "female"`), and `Line.order`'s `local rank = { female = 1, cub = 2, male = 3 }` makes her rank 1, so `local leader = live[1]` is the animal standing at `start`, the rearmost of the file; rank therefore increases with southness and the marching order is the exact reverse of the ground layout; (c) the seeded trail assumes the opposite — `table.insert(seeded, { at = start - toward * back, s = 0 })`, commented "Seeding with the points the followers are ALREADY STANDING ON -- behind her, along the approach".
