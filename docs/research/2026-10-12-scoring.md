# Scoring: what an animal is worth, what a beater is worth, and who is first

2026-10-12, task 160. Builder note, rule 1.

## 1. What the system must do

Karen, verbatim:

* 2026-10-10 (her design doc, answering how a kill is scored): *"How many shoot and male is more
  worth then famale"*.
* 2026-10-11: *"Scoring - yes get money when shoot boar not much but get money and first place ofc
  more legendary more ect"*.
* 2026-10-10: *"beater ranking also is cool"*.

So, three things, and **money is explicitly not one of them today** (Director dispatch, task 160:
money and its saving are step 3b). What is in scope:

1. **A kill is worth what the ANIMAL is worth.** Male above female; a legendary animal above both.
   Today's code scores a kill by HIT ZONE alone -- `POINTS.KILL = { head = 100, chest = 80,
   body = 50, legs = 30 }` -- which task 158 found while writing the start card and wrote down in
   `Match.CONFIG` as a rule waiting for Karen. A zone table cannot express "male is more worth then
   famale" at all: it has no idea what it shot.
2. **A beater scores for an animal he pushed that somebody then shot.** Not for walking; for
   walking animals onto the guns.
3. **A ranking with a first place**, so "first place ofc more" has something to read off.

Numeric targets, which are the whole point of the exercise because every one of them is Karen's to
retune after one drive:

| | target | why this number is the one to beat |
|---|---|---|
| male boar | 10 | the unit of the currency: every other number is read against it |
| female boar | 6 | ~60% of a male: *"male is more worth"*, and a female is still worth shooting |
| cub | 2 | small, and **never zero**, because today's rule is `QUARRY_NOTE`: *"every one of them counts - no cub rule yet"* |
| legendary | 25 | 2.5 male: *"more legendary more"*. **A data row with no animal behind it** |
| beater push | 3 | 30% of a male, paid per animal pushed-and-shot |
| assist | 3 | the same: you moved it or you hurt it, somebody else finished it |
| safety penalty | -10 | one male boar, on top of being tied to a tree |
| a drive's spread | 0-40 | 6 boars, 8-16 players: the board must not be decided by one lucky head shot |

## 2. Sources

Four, all fetched on 2026-10-12 and quoted rather than remembered.

### 2.1 ioquake3, `code/game/g_main.c` -- `SortRanks` / `CalculateRanks`

<https://github.com/ioquake/ioq3> - **GPL-2.0** - maintained (the repo this project already borrowed
the results-scene intermission from, `docs/research/2026-10-12-drive-end-and-results-scene.md`).

Fetched. What it does well, and it is the reason this note exists: **a tie at the top is not a first
place, and the code says so in the data rather than in the drawing.** `qsort(level.sortedClients,
..., SortRanks)` orders the board; then rank is assigned sequentially, first place getting `rank =
0`, and *"we are tied with the previous client"* makes both rows `rank | RANK_TIED_FLAG`. The flag is
also set when `numPlayingClients == 1` -- **a lone player is marked tied, not winning.**

What it does badly for us: nothing, but it has no concept of a support action in the base gametype,
which is source 2.2's job.

### 2.2 ioquake3, `code/game/g_team.h` -- the CTF bonus table

Same repo, same licence. Fetched; the defines verbatim:

```c
#define CTF_CAPTURE_BONUS		100		// what you get for capture
#define CTF_TEAM_BONUS			25		// what your team gets for capture
#define CTF_RECOVERY_BONUS		10		// what you get for recovery
#define CTF_FLAG_BONUS			10		// what you get for picking up enemy flag
#define CTF_FRAG_CARRIER_BONUS	20		// what you get for fragging enemy flag carrier
#define CTF_RETURN_FLAG_ASSIST_BONUS		10	// awarded for returning a flag that causes a capture to happen almost immediately
#define CTF_FRAG_CARRIER_ASSIST_BONUS		10	// award for fragging a flag carrier if a capture happens almost immediately
```

This is the beater rule, already written by somebody else: **a support action is paid only when the
score it enabled happens "almost immediately" after it.** That is exactly `PUSH_WINDOW` -- a
20-second window before the kill -- and it is why a beater who walked past an animal four minutes
ago earns nothing when a hunter shoots it.

What it does badly: the ratio. 10 of 100 is a **tenth** of the scoring action. At a tenth, a beater
would end a ten-minute drive on 1 or 2 points and Karen's *"beater ranking also is cool"* would be a
column of noughts. So the pattern is adopted and the number is not: **30%, not 10%** (section 4).

### 2.3 CIC trophy evaluation of wild boar

<https://jjgomezcaza.es/en/cic-evaluation-and-measurement-of-wild-boar-hunting-trophies.html> -
editorial, no licence - current (the CIC formula is the live international standard).

Fetched. The CIC formula scores *"Length of lower tusks"*, *"Width of lower tusks"*, *"Circumference
of upper tusks"*, their colouring, form, lock and wear, with deductions for asymmetry; European wild
boar medals at 110 / 115 / 120 points.

Cross-checked against the CIC UK & Ireland chapter's own species page
(<https://www.cicukteb.com/uk-species/wild-boar>, fetched): *"medal quality tusks are being observed.
As a rough guide, bronze medal status can be achieved with a lower tusks measurement of at least
25 cm in length."*

What this does well: it settles the direction of Karen's rule from the real sport. **The trophy of a
wild boar IS the tusks** -- every measurement in the formula is a tusk measurement -- and tusks of
that size are a mature male's. The real sport scores the male and does not score the sow at all.

What it does badly, and this is why it is a direction and not a formula: it is a **carcass**
measurement taken after a drying period, on one animal, by a measurer with callipers. A ten-minute
Roblox drive cannot measure a tusk, and a game in which the sow is worth nothing would make half the
animals on the map not worth shooting -- which is the opposite of Karen's `QUARRY_NOTE`. So: male
above female, **female not zero**.

### 2.4 theHunter: Call of the Wild -- trophy rating

Proprietary (Expansive Worlds), live service. **Searched, and the two pages that would quote it are
not readable by any tool here**: the Fandom wiki's `Rating` page answers HTTP 402 and the Steam
Community guide *Trophy Ratings & Fur Types* answers HTTP 429. Recorded as a negative result rather
than cited from memory, which is this repo's rule after
`docs/research/2026-10-05-sounder-march-order.md` caught a search extract attributing a claim to a
page that did not make it.

What survives the failure is only what the search index itself stated, and it is used here as
corroboration and nothing more: in that game a trophy score is a property of the **animal** (antlers,
weight or skull), its sex is a factor, females score lower than males where they score at all, and
**shot placement is not part of the trophy score** -- it feeds a separate integrity/harvest rating.
That is the same split this task is making. It is the weakest of the four sources and no number is
taken from it.

## 3. What is NOT borrowed, and why (rule 2)

Nothing is invented here. Two things are **deliberately not** borrowed:

* **A trophy formula.** No tusk length, no weight, no per-animal roll. One number per kind, in a
  table, as data. Karen retunes a row; nobody retunes a formula.
* **A group/sounder bonus.** `docs/design/drive.md` section 7.1 already settled this (Director item
  L): credit is per boar id, so a beater who pushed a sounder of five and saw three shot is paid
  three times. Unchanged.

## 4. The pattern adopted

**A kill is scored off one data table keyed by the animal's kind; a support action is paid a
fraction of it inside a window before the kill; the board is sorted and ranked as ioquake3 ranks
one, with a tie at the top meaning there is no first place.**

Concretely, and every line of it lives in `Match.CONFIG.POINTS`, which is the one table this game's
score comes out of:

1. `POINTS.KIND` replaces `POINTS.KILL`. `Match.Score.kill` reads `record.kind` -- a field the kill
   record has carried since task 120 (*"WHICH ANIMAL DIED"*) -- and nothing else. **The hit zone is
   retired from scoring and kept everywhere else**: it still decides whether the animal dies
   (`Boar.Wound`), it is still on the kill record, still drawn on the report's silhouette and still
   printed in the report's ZONE column. It simply stops being money. Archived with its reason under
   `backups/`, per rule 7.
2. `POINTS.BEATER_PUSH` replaces `POINTS.PUSH`, and the rule gains ioquake3's *"almost
   immediately"*: the credit that pays is the animal's **last** one before it died. Credit now comes
   from two doors instead of one -- the proximity sampler that already existed, and **the horn**,
   which is the one a 150-stud flush could never reach.
3. `Hud.firstPlaceRank` is `RANK_TIED_FLAG`, read the other way round: it answers "which row is
   first" and answers **nil** when the top two are level, and nil for a single row that has scored
   nothing. A "1st" badge on an all-noughts board would name as winner whoever joined first, because
   that is what `Score.leaderboard`'s last tiebreak (`userId`) does.

**Why 3 and not 1.** A beater's drive is 6 animals at best. At ioquake3's tenth (1 point) the beater
column is noughts and the ranking Karen asked for cannot be read. At 3, a beater who walks four
animals onto the guns (12) is level with a hunter who shot a male and a cub (12) -- the two jobs are
in the same currency, which is the reason `PUSH` existed at all (`Match.Score`'s own header: *"if
only the killer scores then half the players end a ten-minute drive with an empty line"*). It is one
number and `BEATER_PUSH = 0` switches the whole idea off, exactly as `PUSH = 0` did.

**Why the whole table was rescaled and not just the kill row.** `ASSIST` was 10 and `SAFETY_PENALTY`
was -50 against a 100-point head shot. Against a 10-point male, 10 would make an assist worth a
whole animal and -50 would make one safety violation cost five -- more than a good drive earns, so
a single mistake would end a player's board irrecoverably. The old numbers' RATIOS are what Karen
approved, so the ratios are what moved: assist 10% -> 30% of a kill (it was a tenth of a head shot;
it is now the same as a push, which is what an assist is), penalty -50% -> -100% of a kill (it was
half a good kill; it is now one whole animal -- a stronger penalty than before, and still
survivable). **All six numbers are Karen's to overrule** and each is one line.

## 5. What this note does not answer

* **Money.** Karen's *"get money when shoot boar"* is step 3b. The points model is built so money is
  a function of it (a rate per point, or a per-kind price beside the per-kind points) and nothing
  here has to move for that.
* **The cub rule.** *"NOT piglets/cubs"* is in her doc; the code has never had it. `cub = 2` keeps
  today's stated rule honest. The day she protects cubs, that row goes to 0 or negative and
  `QUARRY_NOTE` changes in the same commit -- which is the sentence `Match.CONFIG` already carries.
* **Whether hunters and beaters belong in ONE table.** Built as one (simplest, and it is the one
  board that already exists). A split -- two columns, two firsts -- is a Karen decision; see the
  task report.
* **`docs/design/drive.md` section 7.1 is now stale.** It is the Architect's file and the Builder
  does not write designs (CLAUDE.md roles). Queued in `TASKS.md` for the next Architect run.
