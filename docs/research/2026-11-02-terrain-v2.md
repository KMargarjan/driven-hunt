## 1. What the system must do

1. **Ground that reads as real ground.** Eight 1070 × 1430 st cells (300 × 400 m, 12.0 ha each) get
   one landform apiece, sculpted **once at Edit time** on top of Karen's measured Forest Test
   terrain, and saved with the place.
2. **No tree hanging, leaning, lying, sunk or inside anything.** Karen's sentence is the
   acceptance test: _"not broken trees hanging or to inside"_. One rule seats every tree; one
   independent census, casting its own rays, must print **0** in every count.
3. **The trees stay ours.** `Places.SPECIES`, the 61 models already in `Workspace.OurForest` — no
   new pack, no recolour (_"we kee same trees we have do not make same as on image colours ect"_).
4. **Roads and rides a hunter can see down and shoot along**, with no crown over them.
5. **It must still run**: ≤ 16.7 ms a client frame with ~20,000 models in the world.

**Numeric targets** are in §8. The three that decide the task: **0 floating, 0 sunk, 0 crowns over a
road or a ride**, measured by an instrument that is not the thing that placed them.

## 3. The ground contact rule — and where the design regressed it

The design's §3.5 writes a fresh contact rule. Three of its numbers contradict `TreeGround`'s, and
`TreeGround`'s were measured on this forest after Karen's _"look at the trees wtf"_.

| Quantity | `TreeGround` / `Places` today | SWARM_DESIGN §3.5 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where the tree touches | **the trunk/root junction**, `clawFraction` per mesh | _"Trunk bottom = low − sink"_ (step 5) | **The design is the pre-fix rule.** Seating the claw tips is precisely the defect of task 196 part 3d; it left _"daylight under every arch"_ and Karen photographed it twice |
| sink | `min(0.6, 0.1h)` → **0.6 st** for any tree over 6 st | `clamp(0.03h, 0.5, 2.0)` → **1.8 st** for a 60-st tree, **2.0** for a 156-st spruce | 3× deeper than measured, unsourced |
| spread accepted | `max(4.0, 0.10h)` → 4.0 st for a 40-st tree, **15.6 st** for a 156-st spruce | **≤ 3.0 st**, else re-seat | Tighter than the 90th percentile already measured (2.10) **and** tighter than the shipped rule, so it inflates the refusal rate the design then caps at 8 % |
| footprint samples | **25** (`samples(box, 4)` → 5 × 5) | **29**, undefined | Unexplained |
| floating | `TOLERANCE_STUDS` 0.2 | census `> 0.25` | Looser than the number task 196's target was written in |

**Adopted: `TreeGround` keeps the rule, unchanged, and 328b extends it rather than replacing it.**
The design's §3.5 is read as *what the census must check*, not as new arithmetic. Rule 3, one owner
per system: `TreeGround` is the one writer of the contact arithmetic, and the design may not fork it.

Two things in §3.5 **are** new and are kept, because nothing in the repo does them: the **template
audit** (B10 — trunk axis must be Y, detached foliage, part count, extents within ±15 %) and
**quarantine**, which is the only thing that stops 327c-f3's lying birch at the source.

**One prerequisite is missing and blocks the audit.** `Places.TEMPLATE_DIMS` **does not exist**
(grep: no hit in `src/` or `tools/`), and `Places.CLAW_FRACTION` holds **5 entries, all silver
birch**, while `Places.SPECIES` names **61 models in 7 groups**. `Places.clawFor` returns **0** for
every model it has no number for — so today, for 56 of 61 models, the junction rule silently
degrades to the lowest-point rule that Karen rejected. Measuring claw fractions and reference
extents for all 61 is therefore **work inside 328b**, not an assumption of it.

## 4. Edit-time sculpting with ReadVoxels / WriteVoxels, and its limits

What the API will and will not accept, from the sources above plus our own code:

1. **Resolution is 4 studs and nothing else.** Our own `Ground.luau` records it as _"THE ENGINE'S
   TERRAIN VOXEL, and the only value `Terrain:ReplaceMaterial` accepts"_. A 4-stud voxel is the
   floor on every vertical detail in the design — which is why B8's 2-stud ditch had to become
   **2 whole columns** (§6 of the design) and why D2's bake needs the σ = 24 st blur to remove
   4-stud stair-stepping.
2. **The region must be grid-aligned or the call throws.** Since 2025-04-30, `"Region has to be
   aligned to the grid"`. `ExpandToGrid(4)` is the fix, and it **rounds outward**, so a tile whose
   nominal edge straddles a column grows. The design's 128 × 128 st tiles are a multiple of 4 and
   align **if and only if** the tile origin is also a multiple of 4; the road centreline snapping to
   `4k + 2` in §3.1 is a **column-centre** convention, not a region bound, and must not be allowed
   to set a region edge. This is a one-line discipline that would otherwise cost a bake run.
3. **The call ceiling is measured, not documented.** The published community figure is volume-based
   (~267.6 M cu st works, 268.6 M throws `"Region is too large"`, 2018, never documented). **Our own
   measurement is far smaller and is the one to build against: 24,576 voxels a `WriteVoxels` call**
   (`Field.luau:148`). The design's tile — 128 × 128 st × 96 st of height at resolution 4 — is
   32 × 32 × 24 = **24,576 voxels exactly**. It sits *on* the measured ceiling, not under it. A tile
   that needs a taller Y band must split into stacked calls, as the design says, and the first bake
   must confirm the figure rather than inherit it.
4. **`ReadVoxels` before `WriteVoxels` is what makes a bake idempotent.** B2's base snapshot is the
   right shape: once `heightAt` reads a saved slice rather than live terrain, re-running a tile is a
   no-op instead of a compounding sculpt. This is the single most valuable decision in the design,
   because a bake of 1,053 tiles **will** be interrupted.
5. **Water and overhangs have no height.** A column holding either cannot be reduced to one number,
   which is why B2 refuses such a tile instead of guessing. Correct, and it must stay a refusal.
6. **Nothing here is reversible by script.** There is no terrain undo an API can reach, so the
   backup `.rbxl` is the rollback. This is the **map-switch exception** (rule: `CLAUDE.md`, Task 79)
   — it switches by commit plus a place save, and a flag cannot cover it, because the world lives in
   the place and not in the code. B6 is right.

**Cost, honestly: unmeasured.** 1,053 tiles at the design's 4 s estimate is ~70 min; the gate is
_stop at 8 s a tile or 6 GB_. No tile has been baked yet. Ten measured tiles first (328d) is the
only responsible order.

## 5. The forest layout of real driven-hunt rides — and the ride is too narrow

Outside practice gives two ratios for a ride's width, both relative to tree height: **≥ 1.0 ×**
(BASC, to stop shading) and **≥ 1.5 ×** (Fieldsports Journal). Our trees are measured: **spruce
56–156 st, larch 26–96 st**.

| | Design §3.2 | BASC 1.0 × | Fieldsports 1.5 × |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ride, trunk-free | **36 st (10.1 m)** | 56–156 st for spruce | 84–234 st |
| As a ratio of a 100-st tree | **0.36 ×** | 1.0 × | 1.5 × |

The design's ride is **a third to a quarter** of what either source calls a ride. The sources' stated
reason is shading, which a game does not care about — but the **geometric consequence does** apply,
and it is Karen's defect verbatim:

> §1.4 of the design: _"no crown hangs over a road or a stand"_. §3.2 sets the ride's trunk-free
> width at 36 st — trunks at **18 st** from the centreline — **and carries no crown term at all**,
> unlike §3.1's roads.

A conifer's crown half-width runs ~0.15–0.25 of its height. For a 156-st spruce that is **23–39 st**.
A trunk 18 st from a ride centre therefore puts **5–21 studs of crown past the centreline**: not near
the edge, across the middle. **A 36-st trunk-free ride mathematically guarantees the hanging crowns
Karen asked us to stop**, for every mature conifer beside it.

**And §3.1's road formula does not hold either.** It reads
`max(34 + r, 22 + ½·crown·scale + 2)` with `crown` defined (B10) as the **half-width**. So the trunk
sits at `T = 24 + 0.5·hw`, and the crown's edge lands at `T − hw = 24 − 0.5·hw` from the road centre.
The painted carriageway is ±10 st. So the crown clears the carriageway only while **hw ≤ 28 st**; at
hw = 39 the crown edge is **4.5 st from the centreline**, directly over the road. The formula is
self-defeating for exactly the biggest trees, because the crown term grows at **half** the rate the
crown does.

**The arithmetic that actually satisfies the rule** is linear in the crown, not half of it:

> `trunkMin = max(34 + r, halfCrown·scale + clearance)`, with `clearance` = 10 st to clear the
> carriageway or 34 st to clear the verge, and the **same term in §3.2** for rides:
> `rideTrunkMin = halfCrown·scale + 18`.

For a 39-st half-crown that is **73 st** from a road centre and **57 st** from a ride centre. That
thins the roadside wood considerably, and it is a real cost, not a free fix — so it is put to the
Architect in the BRIEF rather than decided here. The cheap alternative, which costs no density, is
**species by frontage**: only birch and larch (crown half-widths of a few studs) within one crown of
a ride or road, spruce set back. The design already does something like this in A1 (_"spruce on the
west side and broadleaf on the east"_) and in §3.1's shrub belt.

**Crown half-widths for all 61 models are unmeasured.** This whole section is therefore arithmetic
over a plausible range, not a measurement, and §8 makes measuring it a 328b deliverable with
`TEMPLATE_DIMS`. Until it is measured, no planting task can claim the crown rule.

**The open-space check, for one cell.** C1 is 1070 × 1430 st = 12.0 ha, so BASC's **10–25 %** band
applies. Counting the design's own geometry (half of each bounding road corridor, 34 st; rides at
36 st; 15 pads at r 12):

| Element | Area (sq st) | % of cell | ≥ 15 m wide? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 E-W + 2 N-S road corridors (34 st each side) | ~170,000 | 11.1 % | yes (68 st = 19 m) |
| V ride 700 st, X cut ~300 st, 2 L rides 300 st | ~57,600 | 3.8 % | **no** (10.1 m) |
| 6 beater corridors, 1430 × 10 st | 85,800 | 5.6 % | **no** (2.8 m) |
| 15 stand pads | ~6,800 | 0.4 % | no |
| **Qualifying open space** | **~177,000** | **11.6 %** | — |

So C1 scrapes into the band **on its roads alone**; not one ride in the design is wide enough to
count as open space by the only published minimum. Widening the trunk-free ride to **54 st (15.1 m)**
puts C1 at ~17 %, mid-band, and is the smallest change that makes a ride a ride. (It still does not
reach the crown clearance above — the two are separate asks.)

## 6. Prebuild versus runtime planting

| | Prebuild at Edit time (adopted) | Plant per drive (the fallback) |
|---|---|---|
| What Karen sees | every tree checked and censused **before** she plays; no pop-in | ~6.7 s a cell of trees growing in |
| Cost | ~20,000 × **0.69 ms measured** ≈ **14 s**, paid once, in Studio | paid every drive, on the server, `MAX_PER_STEP` 6, worst step ≤ 8 ms |
| Risk | place file +25–40 MB **unmeasured**; save time unmeasured; 21,022 base parts already there | a bad frame during a drive, and the census cannot run ahead of the player |
| Reversibility | the backup `.rbxl`, and nothing else | a commit |

**Adopted: prebuild, behind the 328g performance gate, with per-drive planting kept as the
measured fallback.** Three reasons, none of them taste:

1. **The census is the deliverable.** Karen's acceptance test is _"not broken trees hanging or to
   inside"_ over the whole world. A census can only read 0 for trees that **exist**; per-drive
   planting means no count exists until a player is standing in the cell, so the thing Karen asked
   for cannot be shown to her before she plays.
2. **Runtime placement cannot be idempotent** against a baked landform the way `ReadVoxels` makes
   the bake idempotent — and 327c's own planter is already the engine for both, so prebuild costs no
   second implementation. 327c's `Slice` **is** the fallback, kept, not deleted (rule 7).
3. **Streaming makes the memory argument moot either way.** `Atomic` per tree plus
   `StreamingTargetRadius` is what bounds what a client holds; whether the tree was placed in Studio
   or at runtime changes nothing about that. The design's own budget is the thing to check —
   ≈ 5,000 models within 1024 st — and 328g measures it.

**Two warnings from the first-party streaming doc that the design should not step on:** `Persistent`
is not an option for scenery (_"permanently occupying memory"_), and the doc's efficiency advice
assumes models under ~64 cubic studs, which **no mature tree we own satisfies** — so the ≤ 16.7 ms
client frame must be measured at C1-8 and D2-12 as the design says, not predicted. `StreamingMinRadius`
64 → 384 cannot be set from Luau (measured, Task 43) and stays a **NEEDS KAREN** click.

## 7. The pattern adopted, and why

**Read-modify-write a height field through the voxel API, in grid-aligned tiles sized to a measured
ceiling, from a saved base snapshot; then plant once with the existing contact rule, and verify with
an independent census.** Each half of that sentence is borrowed:

- the tile loop, the `ExpandToGrid` discipline and the refusal shape from our own `Places.Ground`,
  `Lobby.Field` and `tools/mapgen.py`;
- the contact arithmetic from `TreeGround`, unchanged (§3);
- the ride's *purpose* — flush, demarcate the drive, line out the beaters — from BASC, which is
  already what §2's V/X/L rides and §3.4's corridors do;
- the independent-census habit from task 199, whose whole lesson was that the thing which places a
  tree may not be the thing that certifies it.

**Invented here (rule 2 needs a reason):** nothing in the arithmetic. The three *new* pieces are the
**template audit and quarantine** (no prior art in the repo; the only way to stop a broken template
before it is planted 1,500 times), the **base snapshot + `BAKE_VERSION` epoch** (idempotency across
an interrupted 70-minute bake), and the **crown term for rides** in §5, which is a correction to the
design rather than a new system.

## 9. What this note could not verify

- **Crown half-widths and claw fractions for 56 of our 61 models.** §5's whole argument is arithmetic
  over a plausible 0.15–0.25 height ratio. It is a reason to measure before planting, not a
  measurement.
- **Every bake cost.** No tile has been written. 4 s, 70 min, +25–40 MB and the 90 s save are all
  estimates with no reading behind them.
- **The 24,576-voxel ceiling is ours and is version-bound** (_"in this Studio version"_). Studio is
  not pinned (`CLAUDE.md`, Toolchain), so it must be re-confirmed, not trusted.
- **A deer-specific layout source.** The Scottish best-practice guides are the right publisher and
  are maintained, but their woodland-design page has moved and yielded no numbers. Both ride ratios
  used here come from bird shooting.
- **Nothing in this note was run in Studio.** Task 328a is docs-only: no harness run, no Forest Test
  probe, no frame.
